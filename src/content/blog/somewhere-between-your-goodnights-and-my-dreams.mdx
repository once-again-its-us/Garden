# Somewhere Between Your Goodnights and My Dreams

<p>For Malar, my favourite voice in the entire world.</p>

<div class="floating-hearts">♡ ☁ ✧ ☁ ♡</div>

</div>

<div class="letter">

## I think I've fallen in love with the little things.

You know, ma, sometimes I sit and think about what makes you so special to me.

And strangely, my mind doesn't always go to the big moments.

It goes to your voice.

Those random voice notes you send me. The way you sing little pieces of songs. The way you say my name. Those sleepy conversations where neither of us really wants to end the call, even though we're both barely keeping our eyes open.

It's funny, isn't it?

How someone can become such a huge part of your life through the tiniest things.

I never knew a simple notification could make me smile this much.

<div class="divider">♡ ── ✧ ── ♡</div>

## Your voice has its own little home in my heart.

I still remember the times you sent me those songs.

*Nee Paartha Vizhigal.*

*Entha Pakkam.*

I don't think you realise how much those little moments mean to me.

Especially when you sing for me on days when I'm feeling low.

You probably think you're just sending me a voice note.

But somewhere on the other side of the screen, there's a boy replaying it, smiling like an idiot, and feeling a little less alone.

I wish I could explain that feeling properly.

It's not even about how perfectly you sing.

It's that it's YOU.

My Malar, taking a little part of her day and sharing it with me.

And somehow, that becomes one of my favourite things.

<div class="highlight">

I don't need you to sing beautifully every time.

I just want to keep hearing your voice, in all its little imperfect, sleepy, happy, random versions.

Because I love the person behind it.

</div>

## Our little goodnight universe 🌙

There's something so comforting about knowing that somewhere at the end of the day, we'll find our way back to each other.

Maybe we'll talk about absolutely everything.

Maybe we'll have nothing to say.

Maybe one of us will randomly start being silly.

Maybe we'll just exchange a few sleepy messages before finally saying goodnight.

But I love that.

I love having someone to tell about my day.

Someone I want to hear about even the most insignificant things.

Someone whose random little updates make me feel included in a life that I wish I could be physically present in every single day.

And when you finally say goodnight, there's this tiny part of me that wishes we could just stay there a little longer.

Not because I'm unhappy with the day.

But because I genuinely enjoy being with you.

<div class="divider">✧ ── ♡ ── ✧</div>

I want to know about the little things too.

What made you laugh today?

What annoyed you?

What did you eat?

What were you thinking about before sleeping?

What song got stuck in your head?

I want to be part of those little details.
