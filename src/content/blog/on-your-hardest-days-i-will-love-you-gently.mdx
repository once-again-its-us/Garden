# On Your Hardest Days, I Will Love You Gently

<p>A letter for my Malar, from Hirthik.</p>

<div class="floating-hearts">♡ 🌷 ♡</div>

</div>

<div class="letter">

## First, I want you to know something, ma.

I wish I could take away every uncomfortable moment you experience during your periods.

Every cramp, every ache, every restless night, every moment when your body feels like it's fighting against you.

And I know PCOD can make those days complicated in ways that aren't always visible to someone else.

Sometimes it's the physical discomfort. Sometimes it's the unpredictability. Sometimes it's the frustration of not understanding why your body feels different.

And sometimes, I imagine, it's simply exhausting having to deal with everything while still trying to carry on with your ordinary life.

I may not experience exactly what you experience, ma, but I want to understand.

I want to listen.

I want to learn what helps you, what makes things worse, and what you need from me on those days.

Because loving you also means caring about the things you struggle with, even when I cannot personally feel them.

<div class="divider">♡ ── ✧ ── ♡</div>

## If I were beside you on one of those difficult days...

I'd first come close, gently move your hair away from your face and give you a forehead kiss.

I'd ask you how you're feeling.

Not just a casual "Are you okay?"

I'd actually want to know.

Is your lower abdomen hurting? Is your back aching? Are you feeling tired? Do you want something warm? Do you want me to sit beside you or would you rather rest alone for a little while?

And if all you could manage was a little nod or a quiet "I don't know, pa", that's okay too.

You wouldn't have to explain everything perfectly.

I'd bring you water, arrange your pillows, help you get comfortable and make sure you have whatever you usually need nearby.

A warm heating pad if it helps you.

Your favourite snacks.

Something comfortable to wear.

A little blanket.

And me, probably asking every now and then whether you're comfortable.

I might be a little annoying with all my checking, but that's because I love you.

<div class="highlight">

I don't want to simply tell you to take care of yourself.

I want to be someone who makes taking care of yourself a little easier.

</div>

## Let me take care of the little things.

I imagine the two of us having our own little routine for those days.

I'd make sure you don't have to get up unnecessarily when you're exhausted.

I'd help with the food, bring you something you feel like eating, refill your water bottle and keep your essentials within reach.

If you want to sleep, I'd let you sleep.

If you want company, I'd stay.

If you want to complain about how unfair your body is being, I'd listen to every single word.

And if you want to distract yourself, we could watch something silly, talk about random things or just spend some time together.

You don't have to be productive.

You don't have to be cheerful.

You don't have to pretend that the pain isn't bothering you.

For those moments, let the world wait a little.

<div class="divider">✧ ── ♡ ── ✧</div>

## And when all you want is a hug...

Come here, ma.

I'd pull you gently towards me, let you rest against my chest and wrap my arms around you.

I'd kiss your forehead, your cheeks, and the top of your head.

I'd slowly run my fingers through your hair if that feels comforting.

Maybe I'd rub your back gently, or hold your hand while you rest.

If you wanted a gentle massage, I'd ask where it hurts and what kind of touch feels comfortable.

And if physical contact feels uncomfortable that day, I'd respect that too.

I want you to feel safe with me, not obligated to accept affection just because I want to give it.

I want to hold you because you want to be held.

I want to kiss you because you want those kisses.

I want our closeness to feel like comfort, never another thing you have to manage.

<div class="memory-card">

### A little confession ♡

Even on the days when you're curled up in your most comfortable clothes, your hair is messy, you're sleepy and you don't feel particularly pretty...

I will still look at you like you're my favourite girl.

Because you are.

You don't have to look a certain way to be loved by me.

</div>

## About your PCOD, ma...

I know there can be days when PCOD feels frustrating.

The uncertainty, the changes, the appointments, the questions, the things you might worry about but don't always know how to explain.

I never want you to feel that your body is something you have to apologise to me for.

You are not a burden.

You are not less beautiful.

You are not less lovable.

And you certainly don't have to prove that you're strong every single day.

I want to understand your experiences rather than make assumptions about them.

If you want help keeping track of your symptoms, appointments or medication reminders, I'd love to support you.

If you want to talk about something that's worrying you, I'll listen without making you feel silly.

And if you need medical help, I'll support you in getting it.

I won't pretend I can magically fix PCOD with hugs and kisses, ma.

But I can promise to stand beside you while you take care of yourself.

<div class="highlight">

Your body isn't an inconvenience in our relationship.

Your health matters because YOU matter.

</div>

## Little things I want to do for you...

<div class="memory-card">

🌷 Keep your favourite comfort snacks ready.

🌷 Prepare a warm heating pad when you want one.

🌷 Bring you water without making you ask.

🌷 Help you get comfortable with pillows and blankets.

🌷 Give you forehead kisses and gentle cuddles.

🌷 Listen when you feel frustrated or overwhelmed.

🌷 Remind you about your usual medication, if you want me to.

🌷 Accompany you to appointments whenever possible.

🌷 Never make you feel guilty for needing rest.

🌷 Make you laugh when you feel like laughing.

</div>

## A few little letters, just for you...

<details class="reveal">

Come here, ma. I wish I could be beside you right now.

You don't have to do anything for me. Just get comfortable, take the care and rest you need.

I'd sit beside you, hold your hand, give you little forehead kisses and make sure you aren't dealing with everything alone.

If the pain is unusually severe or isn't settling, we'll get you medical help too. I don't want you silently tolerating something that deserves attention.

I love you, my girl. So much.

</details>

<details class="reveal">

You didn't choose to experience these difficulties, and you don't owe anyone an explanation for having them.

You are so much more than a diagnosis, a symptom, a cycle or a difficult day.

I want to grow with you, understand you and support you.

We will take things one step at a time, okay ma?

You don't have to figure everything out tonight.

</details>

I wish you could see yourself through my eyes for just a little while.

You are beautiful when you're dressed up, and you're beautiful when you're wearing your most comfortable clothes.

You're beautiful when you're laughing, when you're sleepy, when you're being silly and when you're simply existing beside me.

You never have to earn my attraction by looking perfect.

I want the real you.

Every version of you.

</details>

Put your head on my chest.

I'll wrap my arms around you, kiss your forehead and stay right here.

Just us.

You can close your eyes, baby. I've got you.

I love you.

</details>

## And one day, when we're living together...

I imagine knowing your little routines so well that caring for you becomes a natural part of our everyday life.

I'll know where you keep your heating pad.

I'll know which snacks you usually crave.

I'll know when you want cuddles and when you need a little space.

I'll learn what makes those days easier for you, and we'll figure things out together.

Maybe I'll be cooking while you rest.

Maybe you'll call me from the bedroom asking for something, and I'll come running only to find that you wanted a hug.

And I'll probably tease you about it before giving you one anyway.

I want those ordinary moments, ma.

I want the responsibility of loving you in everyday life.

Not just the romantic photographs, anniversaries and beautiful outings.

I want the days when you're tired, the days when you're uncomfortable, the days when everything feels a little difficult.

Because you're not someone I want only during the easy parts of life.

I want to share all of it with you.

<div class="memory-card">

### My little promise to you ♡

You never have to apologise for being in pain.

You never have to pretend to be okay.

You never have to feel guilty for needing extra care.

And you never have to be the strong one every single day.

Let me love you gently, too.

</div>

<div class="ending">

<div style="font-size:2.2rem">🌷 🫂 ♡</div>

## On every kind of day, it's still you.

On your happy days.

On your sleepy days.

On your difficult period days.

On the days PCOD makes everything feel complicated.

On the days when you don't feel like yourself.

I want you to know that there's someone who wants to understand, listen, hold your hand and remind you that you aren't alone.

I cannot promise to take away every pain.

But I can promise that your pain will never make me love you any less.

<p class="signature">With the softest hugs and the warmest kisses,</p>

### Your Hirthik.

<p>For my Malar, my favourite girl, always. 💗</p>
