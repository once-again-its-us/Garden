## First things first...

I have a tiny question.

**Do you know what this place is?**

Well, technically, it's a website. Some code, some pages, a little bit of CSS, probably a few bugs waiting to happen.

But that's the boring explanation.

The real explanation?

It's a place where I can leave little pieces of my thoughts, save moments, write things I might otherwise forget, and make something that grows with time.

And yes, you're the reason it exists. ♡
