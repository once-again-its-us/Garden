# The Song You Sang, and the Boy Who Fell a Little More in Love

<p>For the girl whose voice feels like home.</p>

<div class="floating-hearts">🎧 ♡ 🎶</div>

</div>

<div class="letter">

## I wasn't having the best day.

I don't remember every little detail about that particular moment.

But I remember how I felt.

Sometimes, ma, there are days when everything becomes a little too much. Nothing necessarily goes wrong, but you just don't feel like yourself. You want someone to understand without having to explain every little thing.

And then there was you.

You didn't give me some elaborate speech.

You didn't need to find a hundred perfect words.

You sang for me.

A little piece of a song, coming from the person whose voice I already loved hearing.

And somehow, that was enough to make the moment feel different.

<div class="divider">♡ ── ✧ ── ♡</div>

## Nee Paartha Vizhigal.

I wonder whether you realise how much I treasure that little voice note.

To you, it might have been a simple moment of singing for your boyfriend.

But to me?

It was something I wanted to hold on to.

I could hear you through my phone, and for those few minutes, I wasn't thinking about anything else.

I was just listening.

Listening to your voice, your little expressions, the way you sang those lines.

And I remember thinking how incredibly lucky I was to have someone who would share something so personal with me.

You probably don't even know how many times little things like that stay with me.

I don't just hear a song anymore.

I remember you.

<div class="highlight">

There are songs I listen to because I like their melodies.

And then there are songs I listen to because somebody I love once sang them for me.

You turned a song into a memory, ma.

</div>

## And then came Entha Pakkam...

Another little piece of you.

Another voice note that I wish I could keep somewhere safe forever.

You know what I find beautiful?

I never needed a professional singer.

I never needed a perfect recording.

I never needed a performance.

I just wanted to hear you.

Your voice, exactly as it is.

The little imperfections, the natural pauses, the way you express yourself.

I love all of it.

And if someday we're sitting together in our home and you randomly start humming one of these songs, I hope you know I'll probably stop whatever I'm doing just to listen.

You might laugh at me.

You might ask why I'm staring.

But what am I supposed to do, ma?

My favourite person is singing right in front of me.

Of course I'm going to listen.

<div class="divider">✧ ── ♡ ── ✧</div>

## Something I wish I could tell the girl behind that voice note.

Thank you.

Thank you for singing when I wasn't feeling my best.

Thank you for sharing something that came from you.

Thank you for making me feel cared for without making it a big occasion.

I know you have your own difficult days too.

Your own thoughts, worries, responsibilities and little battles.

And I hope you know that you never have to be the person who fixes everything for me.

I don't want you to feel responsible for making every bad day disappear.

Sometimes I just want you beside me.

Sometimes your voice is enough.

Sometimes a random little message from you makes my entire day better.

And sometimes, I simply want to listen to you talk about absolutely nothing.

<div class="memory-card">

### A little secret ♡

If you ever wonder whether your little efforts matter to me, remember this:

I still think about the songs you sang for me.

That's how much those little moments mean.

</div>

## A future I keep imagining...

Imagine a quiet evening in our future home.

Maybe you're sitting beside me, doing something on your laptop.

I'm working on something of my own.

And then, out of nowhere, you start humming.

I look up.

You continue singing, pretending not to notice me listening.

I eventually come sit beside you.

Maybe I'll ask you to sing that particular song again.

Maybe you'll refuse just to tease me.

And I'll keep asking until you finally give in.

Then I'll hold you close and listen.

No screen recording.

No waiting for the voice note to download.

No distance between us.

Just you, me, and a song that once travelled through a phone to reach me.

The day, the feeling, the song, your voice.

Because sometimes the most precious memories aren't the ones where we're doing something extraordinary.

They're the ones where someone makes us feel loved without even realising how much they're doing.

You did that for me, ma.

And I hope I get to spend a lifetime returning that same feeling to you.
