# The Night I Wanted Time to Stop

<p>October 18, 2026 · Written with love, by Hirthik</p>

<div class="floating-hearts">♡ ✧ ♡</div>

</div>

<div class="letter">

There are some days that pass by like any other day.

And then there are days that quietly become a part of you. Days that you don't want to forget, not because something extraordinary happened, but because, for a little while, everything felt exactly where it was supposed to be.

**October 18 was one of those days.**

I don't think I'll ever be able to explain what it feels like to have someone you've loved for so long right there beside you, sharing a moment that once existed only in your imagination.

For the longest time, you were someone I missed, someone I waited for, someone I wished I could hold close.

And then there was this little moment of ours.

You and me.

Under the same night sky.

<div class="divider">✧ ── ♡ ── ✧</div>

## If I could keep one little piece of that night...

I'd keep the feeling.

The feeling of being close to you. Of having a memory that belonged to nobody else but us. Of looking at the person who means so much to me and realising that this isn't just something I'm imagining anymore.

It's us, ma.

The same two people who once had so many things to say through screens, now making memories that we could actually carry home with us.

I wish I could put that entire night inside a little glass jar.

Not just the beautiful parts. Everything.

The tiny moments, the warmth, the closeness, the way an ordinary night became something I would want to revisit a thousand times.

<div class="highlight">

If somebody asked me what my favourite place in the world was, I think I'd have a very simple answer.

Anywhere, as long as you're there.

</div>

## A little note for you, Malar

I hope you understand something, ma.

I don't love our memories just because they're beautiful.

I love them because they're ours.

I love that we get to have little stories that nobody else knows the meaning of. I love that someday, when we're older, we can look back at these photographs and laugh about how young we were.

And I hope we'll have hundreds of nights like this.

Different places. Different skies. Different versions of us.

But still you and me.

<div class="divider">♡ ── ✧ ── ♡</div>
